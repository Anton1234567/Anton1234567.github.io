About Me

I'm a recent graduate in game development with a focus on programming. My experience comes primarily from Unity and C#. I'm passionate about creating engaging, functional experiences and continuously expanding my technical skills.

What drives me the most is problem solving, finding creative solutions to complex problems, and constantly learning something new. During my education, I worked on several games both individually and as part of larger teams.

I've made three games for school courses, participated in several game jams, and released five games on itch.io. I'm also currently working on a multiplayer game with a friend in my free time.
